# Product

## Register

product

## Users

B2B Marketing / Sales Technology professionals at Demandbase. Today: a single power user (the builder). Near-term: a small internal team of marketing and sales colleagues who did not build it, arriving via link with no onboarding call. They live in this app during work mornings — scanning news, pulling briefings, generating enablement content — often between meetings. The interface must be self-explanatory to a first-time teammate while staying fast and dense for the daily power user.

## Product Purpose

B2B MarTech Intel aggregates industry news (RSS, Google News, NewsAPI), analyzes market trends with AI, and generates brand-compliant sales-enablement and thought-leadership content. Success = a user opens it each morning, understands market movement in minutes, and can produce a briefing, battle card, or deck without leaving the app. It replaces a scatter of newsletters, ChatGPT tabs, and slide templates with one intelligence workbench.

## Brand Personality

Polished, intelligent, calm. Target finish: Linear/Notion-grade product craft — refined spacing, quiet chrome, purposeful motion, delight in details rather than decoration. It should feel like a professional intelligence tool that happens to be beautiful, not a dashboard demo. Demandbase brand accents (Midnight, Sky, Sunset) carry identity; the surface stays composed.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS dashboard slop: gradient text, glassmorphism cards, hero-metric tiles, identical icon-card grids.
- Cluttered "everything-visible" admin panels where every page shouts equally.
- Prototype tells: default shadcn gray-on-gray with no hierarchy, unstyled empty states, spinner-only loading, toast spam.

## Design Principles

1. **Morning-scan first.** The primary job is rapid comprehension of what changed. Hierarchy, scannability, and information density beat decoration on every screen.
2. **Self-explanatory for the second user.** Every page must make sense to a teammate seeing it cold: clear titles, purposeful empty states, no builder-only jargon.
3. **One system, eleven pages.** Shared patterns (page headers, filters, cards, loading/empty/error states) must be consistent across all routes — no page drifts into its own dialect.
4. **AI output is the product.** Generated briefs, trends, and decks deserve the best typography in the app; markdown rendering quality is core UX, not an afterthought.
5. **Quiet chrome, confident accents.** Navigation and controls recede; Demandbase accent color marks the primary action and live/AI activity, nothing else.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensible-defaults bar: WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text), full keyboard operability, visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation, dark/light parity. No formal compliance mandate; AA violations are flagged as fix-worthy findings, not blockers.
